A Party for a Cat?

From Chapter Two of Dreamers Are Welcome

A party for a cat? Granda called the idea preposterous and, when Mrs van Erbel ignored her concerns, upset the neat lines of biscuits on the supper platter. But the Madame did not notice the misalignment of the petit fourres, so engrossed was she in the poster she was painting, and Granda seethed in the corner for an hour before realising she was only upsetting herself. It seemed today even ladies of quality did not notice the detail, she decided, realigning the biscuits with relief. They only observed bends in a cat’s whiskers or a tableau of steppes in his sun-lit fur.

A party for a cat! Granda spat in the sink when she was excused then spent the next twenty minutes scouring it. It never took long to rid herself of the instant anger she felt in her powerlessness, it was the slow anger that remained. A party it would be. She would make sure it would be the best for her Madame, because that was her job. But, returning to the living room she found Mrs van Erbel dancing under a tickertape shower, despite her knee injury, with the kitten. She had the urge to say to hell with it all, to climb to her room for an unheard-of on-duty pipe. “Who is cleaning this?” she raged. “Not you! Not that hairball cat!”

Mrs van Erbel chortled then announced the party manifesto. “Boring people are not welcome. The snide, the gossipy, the bland, the perfectly controlled. I’ve had a lifetime of them! We need a bit of fun!” She smiled and Granda lit up inside until she realised her Madame was talking to the cat.
