James Barker

Author

James Barker was born sometime between the 60s and the 80s, although he is unsure of the century.

Raised in the English Midlands, he has since lived all over Europe, befriending all manner of strays wherever he goes.

He is interested in parties and unreliable memories and enjoys dancing in kitchens at all hours of the day and night. He holds a PhD in Creative Writing from Loughborough University and a Masters degree from Lancaster University.

Dreamers Are Welcome is his first novel.
